# Newsboat

• ^e s — stars  • ^e p — publishes  
• \\ — group     • RIGHT — open  
• U — urlview   • ^u — source   
• r — set read  • ^b — bookmarks  
• j/k — prev/next unread  
• \[/\] — prev/next-unread-feed  

# Macros  
• ,p & ,P — show images/save images  
• ,y & ,Y — show links/hide links   
• ,R & ,r — show/hide read feeds  
• :tag — only show feeds w/tag  
• :goto — Search for a feed
